    How It Works

    STEP 1

    Connect your wallet

    Any BNB Chain wallet works for paid spins. Holders of a Binance Agent ID also get one free spin, once per wallet.

    STEP 2

    Spin — one transaction

    Pay the current step price (starting at 0.0008 BNB) and the machine does the rest. One transaction, one capsule. Max 25 spins per wallet.

    STEP 3

    Tier reveals instantly

    Your capsule's tier is decided and minted in that same transaction. If yours was the 100th paid spin, the 0.025 BNB jackpot arrives in it too.

    FAQ

    What is a spin?

    One spin mints one TalvBot capsule NFT to your wallet and reveals its tier — Common, Rare or Legendary — inside the same transaction. There is no reveal event later and nothing to open afterwards; the capsule you get is final the moment the transaction confirms.

    What does a spin cost?

    The price steps up as the machine empties: 0.0008 BNB for the first 250 capsules minted, then 0.0010, 0.0012, and 0.0015 BNB for the final stretch (capsules 751–999). The panel always shows the live price from the contract — quote it before you spin, because the contract only accepts the exact current price.

    How does the jackpot work?

    Every 100th paid spin — paid spin #100, #200, all the way to #900 — wins 0.025 BNB, sent by the contract to the spinner inside the winning spin transaction itself. That is 9 jackpots across the whole supply. There is no claim function anywhere in the contract: if you won, the BNB is already in your wallet.

    Who gets the free spin?

    Wallets holding a Binance Agent ID get exactly one free spin, once per wallet, ever. Free spins mint from the same 999-capsule pool and roll Common (75%) or Rare (25%) — never Legendary — and they do not count toward the jackpot counter.

    How many spins can one wallet make?

    25 spins per wallet, total — free and paid combined. The cap is enforced by the contract.

    What happens at sellout?

    When capsule #999 is minted, the machine is empty and spinning closes for good. All 999 capsules — and all 9 jackpots — are settled by then, because every reward was paid inside its own spin transaction. Nothing is left pending, for anyone.
